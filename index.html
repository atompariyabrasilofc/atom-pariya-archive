ACTING · FIRST WORK · MAY–JUNE 2018

Social Death Vote

Role: Best · One of the drama's central characters

Original broadcast: Channel 3 (CH3), with episodes also available on LINE TV

Format: 6 episodes · released weekly on Saturdays

The drama explores the harmful effects of social media on teenagers, including cyberbullying, psychological violence, school bullying and crimes committed by minors. It also examines the consequences of these actions for both those involved and their victims.
